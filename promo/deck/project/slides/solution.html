What Roar does

Every phone and pair of glasses in the stands becomes one camera network.

01

Capture

Fans film from their seat on a phone or smart glasses. Only their section is attached, never GPS.

02

Understand

AI agents on Microsoft Foundry read each clip, match it to the live game and draft a Moment.

03

Share and protect

Fans everywhere relive it from every angle. SOS reports reach the control room in seconds.

Three steps. Capture from the seat. Understand with AI agents on Microsoft Foundry. Then share the moment with every fan, and give the stadium a faster safety channel.
