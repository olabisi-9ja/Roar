This is one real run against the live app. A fan filmed the stand behind the goal. In about 14 seconds the AI labelled it a celebration at 85% confidence and matched it to the 61st-minute equaliser: “Section C erupts after Okoro’s 61’ strike”. A moderator published it, and the goal in the For You feed now plays the fan’s clip, tagged Fan angle.
