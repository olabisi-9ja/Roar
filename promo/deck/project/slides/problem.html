The problem

A stadium full of cameras. None of them connected.

Fans far away

Fans who can’t be there get the broadcast angle, not the atmosphere.

Angles lost

The best views of a goal stay scattered across thousands of camera rolls.

Stands unseen

Security can’t watch every seat, and a fan in trouble has no direct line to a steward.

Every match day the stands hold thousands of cameras. Today that footage goes nowhere useful: fans at home miss the atmosphere, the best angles stay private, and the control room still relies on what its own cameras and stewards can see.
