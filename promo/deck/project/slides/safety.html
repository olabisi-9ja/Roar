A fan holds the SOS button for two seconds, picks the emergency and types their section and row. Medical and fire reports are critical straight away; the Safety Analyst refines the rest. The control room sees it at the top of the list, acknowledges it and assigns a steward, and the fan’s screen updates live: seen by the control room, then a steward on the way.
